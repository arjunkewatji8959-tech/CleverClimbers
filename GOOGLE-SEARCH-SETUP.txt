GOOGLE SEARCH + LOGO SETUP
==========================

This project now includes:
- favicon.ico and favicon.png from the CleverClimbers logo
- favicon links on HTML pages
- Organization JSON-LD with the official logo URL
- robots.txt with sitemap URL
- sitemap.xml
- canonical and Open Graph metadata on the homepage

IMPORTANT:
Google does not guarantee that a favicon/logo will appear immediately in search results.
The website must be live, crawlable and indexed. Google chooses when and where to show it.

AFTER UPLOAD / DEPLOYMENT:
1. Open Google Search Console: https://search.google.com/search-console/
2. Add/verify the property: https://cleverclimberindia.com/
3. Open Sitemaps and submit: sitemap.xml
4. Open URL Inspection.
5. Enter: https://cleverclimberindia.com/
6. Click Request Indexing.
7. Repeat URL Inspection for important pages if needed.
8. Keep the favicon at the website root and do not block it in robots.txt.

For the favicon, Google recommends a crawlable square icon. This package contains a 512x512 PNG and ICO.
Search appearance can take time; requesting indexing is not a guarantee of immediate inclusion.
